Your benefits. Your deadlines. No paywall.

Watchstander is a free app for active duty, Guard and Reserve, retirees, veterans, and military spouses. It is the companion to the Watchstander Benefits Brief newsletter, built by a retired Coast Guard Chief Storekeeper.

No subscription. No sign-in. Your dates and numbers stay on your phone.

Getting Out checklist and deadline tracker

Enter your separation or retirement date and Watchstander lays out what is due and when, with an optional reminder a week before each date. It is built from five newsletter guides:

Transition timeline
Transferring your GI Bill while you are still in
Keeping life insurance with VGLI
The Survivor Benefit Plan decision
Retiree TRICARE vs. TAMP vs. CHCBP

Also inside

Plain-English explainers on TSP and BRS, SGLI, and PCS moves, each linked to official sources. A money check-up with every assumption spelled out. Discounts we confirmed on each company's own page. Installation basics and state veteran benefits.

Real help, for free

For VA claims, Watchstander points you to accredited Veterans Service Organizations, which help you file at no cost. We never charge for claim help and never refer you to anyone who does.
