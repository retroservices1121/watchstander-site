Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026.

The short version is that Watchstander does not collect your personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your role, your dates, the checklist items you mark done, and the numbers you enter in the money check-up are saved only on your device. There is no account or sign-in, and we have no server that receives this information. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics or ad tracking, we do not sell or share data, and we do not ask for your location.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off any time in iOS Settings.

App updates

The app checks with Expo, our update service, for content updates. That request carries standard technical information like the app version and device type, not your personal data.

Links to other sites

Watchstander links to official sources and to businesses that offer discounts. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Anything sponsored will be clearly labeled.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Children

Watchstander is not directed at children under 13.
