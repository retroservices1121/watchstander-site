Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Watchstander has no accounts or sign-in, and we have no app server that receives your entries. The services that deliver updates and display maps receive limited technical information. We also receive anything you choose to email us.

What stays on your phone

Watchstander saves your role, dates, checklist, Money Check-Up inputs and settings on your device. On Android, Watchstander disables cloud backup for its app data. Some Android devices can still transfer app data directly to a new phone.

In Settings, under Privacy, choose “Erase my data on this phone” to clear your saved entries and settings and cancel scheduled reminders. Uninstalling Watchstander also removes its local app data from that phone.

What we do not do

Watchstander has no separate analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs and does not use advertising identifiers. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. Turn them off using Deadline reminders in Watchstander Settings or in your device’s notification settings on iOS or Android.

App updates

Watchstander automatically checks for updates through Expo’s EAS Update service. The service receives technical information such as a randomly generated install identifier, the app version used as the runtime version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the IP address the request comes from. The update check uses this information to deliver updates. It can also include a short error report if a previous update failed to launch. Erasing your data in Settings leaves this install identifier in place. Uninstalling the app removes it from the phone. Update checks use HTTPS. See Expo’s privacy policy.

Maps

When enabled in the Android build, the installation map in Discounts uses the Maps SDK for Android. If the Android map is disabled, Discounts shows a list of installations instead. On iOS, the map uses Apple Maps. Google may receive device and usage information needed to display the map. See Google’s privacy policy.

Watchstander does not request location permission or send your location. Map pins use coordinates bundled with the app.

Map search links open Apple Maps on iOS or Google Maps on Android with the business name. For searches near an installation, the link includes its city and state on Android or its map coordinates on iOS. Android map search links use HTTPS. Google describes how it protects Maps data in its own documentation.

Links to other sites

Watchstander links to official sources and to businesses that offer discounts. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Anything sponsored will be clearly labeled.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you send and use them to reply. You can ask us to delete those emails at watchstander.app@gmail.com.

Children

Watchstander is not directed at children under 13.
